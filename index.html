About KwaMashu

Rooted in
community.
Built for what’s next.

KwaMashu is one of Durban’s largest and most vibrant townships, located north of the city centre. Established in the late 1950s, it has grown into a thriving community known for its entrepreneurial spirit, local businesses, sporting talent and strong community identity.

Today, KwaMashu continues to grow through its businesses, schools, organisations and entrepreneurs. Notable people such as Henry Cele and many other influential South Africans have roots in KwaMashu.

From bustling markets and local talent to a deep sense of belonging, KwaMashu is a community built on people who look out for one another.
